Valour House has a new interest group for everyone who loves games, and this website is its new home.

## One community, different games

VH E-Sports is for students who want to play, learn and connect across every kind of game. From mobile to PC to console, the community is built for every platform, play style and skill level. You do not need to be competitive, and you do not need to play everything: come for the game you enjoy and the people you want to play it with.

We are starting with these games:

- **Mobile:** Mobile Legends: Bang Bang and Honor of Kings
- **PC:** VALORANT and Counter-Strike 2
- **Console:** Super Smash Bros. Ultimate, EA SPORTS FC 26, NBA 2K and Overcooked! 2

## What is on the website

- **[News](/news):** announcements, event write-ups and results.
- **[VHCG](/vhcg):** everything about the Valour House Cluster Games E-Sports competition, including the rules, schedule, standings, match records and the detailed MLBB and VALORANT rulebooks.
- **[Player Toolkit](/toolkit):** a collection of practical online tools for players. If you are newer to FPS games, try the MSLAB aim trainer to test your sensitivity and find a setup that feels right.
- **[Committee](/committee):** the people running VH E-Sports, and how to reach them.

## Join us

Session announcements, event updates and tournament information are all shared in our Telegram group. [Join the community](/join) and say hello.
